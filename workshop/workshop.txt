description=[list]
description=[*]Create, rename and reorder folders.

description=[*]Switch sections without losing pending setting edits.
description=[*]Keep folder assignments when a mod is disabled and later returns.
description=[*]Local layout with a previous-valid backup.
description=[*]English and Russian interface; Unicode folder names.
description=[*]Compact navigation for narrow windows and gamepad controls.
description=[/list]
